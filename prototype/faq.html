Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about the program.

General

What is The Technical Collective?
The Technical Collective is a community-led program that connects small to mid-sized nonprofits with junior Salesforce administrators seeking real-world experience, supported by seasoned Salesforce professionals.
Who is this program for?
This initiative is ideal for nonprofits using Salesforce, certified junior admins looking to gain hands-on experience, and experienced Salesforce professionals who want to mentor and give back.
Is there a cost to participate?
No, the program is designed to provide pro-bono support to nonprofits, while offering a skills-building pathway for junior admins.
What kinds of Salesforce projects are supported?
Some of our past projects covered report and dashboard creation, process updates, data clean-up, documentation, and basic automation. We are open to other products or types of projects based on availability and skillset.
How are projects scoped and managed?
Each project needs to have a clear, achievable and limited scope, and includes oversight from an experienced Salesforce professional to ensure quality and alignment with best practices. Please refer to our Promises & Expectations section for more information.

For nonprofits & charities

How can our nonprofit apply for support?
You can complete the online application form detailing your Salesforce setup, current challenges, and support needs. We will review and match you with an appropriate team.
What are the eligibility criteria for nonprofits?
Your organisation should be using Salesforce Nonprofit Cloud or similar, ideally using no more than the 10 donated licenses provided through the Power of Us Program from Salesforce.org, and be open to collaboration and knowledge-sharing.
Will we be working directly with a junior admin?
Yes. Your assigned junior admin will take the lead on the work, but all tasks are reviewed and supported by a senior Salesforce technical expert.
What are the expected time commitments for nonprofits?
This depends on project scope. You will need to commit to a brief onboarding session and regular check-ins.
What if our needs are too complex for a junior admin?
We will assess your requirements during the scoping process. If a project is too advanced, we may refer you to a more appropriate support channel or re-scope the task.

For junior Salesforce professionals

What do I need to join as a junior professional?
You need to be already upskilled in Salesforce by earning at least one certification, and be seeking work experience to add to your CV or resume.
What kind of support will I receive?
You will be paired with an experienced Salesforce mentor who will guide you through best practices, review your work, and provide feedback.
Will this experience count toward job applications or references?
Yes. You will receive documented project experience and the option to request a reference from your technical expert or the nonprofit after successful completion.
Do I get paid?
This is a voluntary learning program and is currently unpaid. However, the experience is highly valuable and can significantly boost your employability. We are open to paid offerings in the future based on availability, length of project, and complexity.
How long is each placement?
Due to the nature of volunteer work, we are not able to guarantee a project length. This will be decided during the project kick-off, when scope is established and ways of working are defined.

For technical experts

How can I get involved as a mentor or technical expert?
You can register via our website to express interest. We will then match you with junior professionals or projects aligned with your skills and availability.
What's expected of me as a mentor?
You will offer technical guidance, review the junior professional's work, answer questions, and ensure deliverables align with Salesforce best practices. We match a team of experts to ensure there is always a technical expert available for support.
Do I need nonprofit experience to be a mentor?
No, but familiarity with Nonprofit Cloud and/or NPSP is helpful. The key requirement is at least 5 years of hands-on Salesforce experience, and a passion for supporting others is essential.
Can I pick which projects or professionals I support?
Yes. We try to match you based on your interests and availability, and you will have the opportunity to accept or decline any proposed engagement.
