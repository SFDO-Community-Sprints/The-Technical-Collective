For junior professionals

Gain real experience, with mentorship

You are a certified Salesforce professional looking for an exciting opportunity to gain hands-on experience and make a difference in the nonprofit sector.

Get involved with The Technical Collective and become part of our mission to support charities and their impactful missions. As a junior Salesforce professional, you'll have the chance to work directly with nonprofits, implementing Salesforce solutions and honing your technical skills.

But it's not just about technical expertise — you'll also develop valuable soft skills and receive guidance from experienced technical coaches. This is your chance to give back to the community, make a meaningful impact, and enhance your professional profile. Join us and embark on a rewarding journey of learning, growth, and making a difference.

Eligibility

We are seeking junior Salesforce professionals who meet the following requirements:

  • ✓ A relevant Salesforce Certification
  • ✓ Desire to gain hands-on experience and the time to commit
  • ✓ Completed the relevant Super Set, or created a Salesforce portfolio / dev org project
  • ✓ Enthusiastic and driven to take your career to the next level

What we ask of you

  • ✓ Show your Salesforce commitment with any projects you've built to learn so far
  • ✓ Be willing to learn and open to feedback
  • ✓ Embrace a learning mindset and don't hesitate to seek help
  • ✓ Dedicate the time to stick to project timelines
  • ✓ A closing survey and case study on the project's impact for your career
  • ✓ Sign and return kick-off documents, such as promises and an NDA

What you can expect from us

  • ✓ Real work experience, applying and enhancing your skills in a live setting
  • ✓ Exposure to a nonprofit's mission and a chance to make an impact
  • ✓ Mentorship and guidance from senior Salesforce technical experts
  • ✓ Networking within the Salesforce and nonprofit communities
  • ✓ Access to best practices, architectural frameworks and templates
  • ✓ Develop business, problem-solving and critical-thinking skills
  • ✓ Practice and improve soft skills like communication and requirement-gathering
