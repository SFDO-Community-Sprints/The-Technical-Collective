What is The Technical Collective?

It was born from an age-old problem in the Salesforce ecosystem. Junior admins don't have enough experience to land their first role. Nonprofits have donated Salesforce licenses but need help getting started or maintaining their systems. And technical experts want to support — but lack the structure to volunteer their time.

A group of us came together at the Salesforce Commons January 2023 sprint and built a program that solves all three at once.

Who can participate?

Three audiences, each giving and getting something they need.

🏛️

Nonprofits & Charities

Receive pro-bono or compensated support on a scoped piece of Salesforce work — process updates, reports & dashboards, or documentation. For small- to mid-sized orgs on Power of Us donated licenses.
