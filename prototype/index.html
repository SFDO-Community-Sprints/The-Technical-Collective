One problem, three sides

The Technical Collective was born from an age-old problem in the Salesforce ecosystem — where each group has exactly what another is missing.

Junior admins can't get a start

Certified but stuck — without real-world experience, landing that first role is hard.

Nonprofits need help

Donated Salesforce licenses, but limited resources to set them up or maintain them.

Experts want to give back

Seasoned pros are ready to mentor — they just need the structure to do it well.

We bring all three together: juniors gain experience, nonprofits get support, and experts share their craft.
